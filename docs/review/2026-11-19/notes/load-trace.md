## Shared boundaries used by other platforms

- Detection and manifest registration (`datasetFactory.ts`, `datasetManifest.ts`, `datasetFiles.ts`, `folderListing.ts`): every platform.
- Instance cache and disposal (`datasetCache.ts`: `retainDataset`, `dropDatasets`, keep two unused): every platform.
- `AnnDataH5adDataset`: H5AD, AnnData Zarr, Icechunk, standard Visium (`createVisiumDataset`), and the tables of SpatialData, Atera and MERSCOPE (`analysis/roiLimit.repro.ts` relies on this).
- H5 worker (`h5WorkerFile.ts`, `src/workers/h5*`): H5AD, Xenium `cell_feature_matrix.h5`, Visium and Visium HD matrices, Feature Slice.
- Load commit and staleness (`whenCommitted`, `isCurrentDatasetLoad`, `datasetAbortRef`, `startLatestRequest`): every platform's first paint and every workflow hook.
- Rendering (`useViewerLayerData`, `ViewerCore`, `MorphologyCanvas`, `MorphologyTileStore`): every platform.

## Leads from the trace

- **Detection runs twice on a cold load:** once in `startDatasetLoad` and again in `createSpatialDataset` (`datasetFactory.ts:1540`). `detectSpatialDataset` has no memo of its own. The probes may be served from the HTTP cache. Count the requests in stage 6 before reporting it.
- **The reader does not receive the load's signal.** `getSpatialDataset` takes no signal, so a superseded load's reads continue. This is known: `open-items.md`, "Cancelling a load…". Measure it in stage 5.
